Rules (this shipped, replacing the gauge-plus-cup pair above). The mark is ONE glyph, the coffee cup (SF Symbol cup.and.saucer/cup.and.saucer.fill), never two side by side. Keep-awake ON draws the whole cup — rim and liquid — in the awake tint; OFF draws it as a plain template image, white on dark and black on light, exactly like every other stock menu-bar icon, and buys the system's automatic tinting for free. The cup's fill level is the fleet's capacity, readyCount / enabledCount, clipped to the bottom fraction of the outline: empty at 0, full at 1. Colour still carries urgency, but now on the cup itself rather than on a number beside it, and the same precedence as the numeric label always used — a poll that did not answer draws a red outline with no liquid (there is nothing to show a fraction of), "none ready, something near" draws amber, and everything else follows keep-awake. An all-disabled fleet draws an empty cup, not a fault colour: that is an operator's decision, not a poll failure. The cup itself is now the default view of capacity, and the 9/13 label repeats the identical fact in words for an operator who wants it spelled out. Open item, flagged rather than changed here: the bridge asks for that label to default OFF now that the cup carries the fact on its own; the mechanism (MenuBarCountsPreference's absent-key default) sits outside this feature's file list and is left at its shipped default (ON) pending that call. The tooltip is unchanged: one sentence, with units, updated every poll.
